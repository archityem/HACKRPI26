Product: Tripflow, a no-login group trip planner.
Live: https://hackrpi-26.vercel.app
Repo: https://github.com/archityem/HACKRPI26
Production branch: main. Branch off main. Do not push or merge to main unless Archit said so.

What it is:
- One person starts a trip and sends a link. Friends join with a first name. No account and no password.
- A device token is the credential. Budgets stay private; the group sees a range.
- Screens: Invite, Answers, Vibe, Rank, Plan. Rank is Gavin's Ticket Stack. Drag to reorder, then the top pick becomes the plan.
- Gemini suggests destinations, prices a place someone adds, and drafts the itinerary. If the key is missing or the call fails, the app uses sample destinations.
- Upstash Redis in production. JSON file locally. Deployed on Vercel.
